WaveshareMon Web Flasher

Flash the glucose monitor firmware for the Waveshare 1.54″ e-paper boards straight from your browser. Pick the card that matches your panel: the flasher tells the ESP32-S3 and the ESP32-C6 apart by itself.

This browser can't flash devices. Web Serial is required — use Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge on a desktop computer (Windows, macOS or Linux). Firefox and Safari are not supported, and neither are phones or tablets.

Before you start

  • Works in Chrome or Edge on any desktop OS. Use a USB data cable.
  • The boards use the chip's native USB: no driver is needed. If no serial port shows up, hold the BOOT button while plugging in the cable (download mode), then try again.
  • Choosing Erase device wipes saved settings and Bluetooth bonds — recommended for a first install, not needed for re-flashing.

After flashing

  • The device advertises as WaveshareMon-XXXX for 10 minutes after every start (and permanently until it has been configured). Open the WaveShareMon Android app, tap Scan, pick the device, accept the pairing prompts, choose the data source and Write.
  • Sources: xDrip / AAPS through the phone (Bluetooth), xDrip Mi Band (Bluetooth, no app needed afterwards), Nightscout, Dexcom Share or LibreLinkUp (Wi-Fi).
  • Once configured the device sleeps between readings and wakes every 5 minutes. Its USB port is gone while it sleeps: press PWR (wakes it) or hold BOOT for 3 s (setup mode, 10 minutes awake) before flashing again.
